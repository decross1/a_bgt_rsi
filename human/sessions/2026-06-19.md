## The roadmap (approved 2026-06-19)

Get to the apparatus's payload — **novel idea → research pipeline → human go/no-go →
applied tier** — fast, demonstrated by **three loops**: (1) **falsification** (kill a
known-refutable hypothesis), (2) **rediscovery** (a known idea flagged + not progressed),
(3) **full novel → classical experiment → synthetic → bubble → cockpit go/no-go →
applied (paper) → interactive applied**. Plus the 4-page reframe: Dashboard = health +
in-flight research; `/todo` = sign-off to applied-tier; Activity = deep-dive on running
processes; Coordinator = orchestration **history**; Experiments = interactive applied
refinement + a card wall (click → full lifecycle + findings). Sequencing: **S1** =
ref-passing + Loops 1 & 2 + cheap page wins; **S2–3** = Loop 3 semi-manual; **S4+** =
automate the closed loop.
