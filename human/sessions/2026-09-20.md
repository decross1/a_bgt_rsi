# 2026-09-20 — Research progression and proposal oversight

## Scope

Repair the path from exploratory game-theory research to a registered, replayable
study. Preserve historical records and current model serving. Keep future Oracle
agendas pending exact-revision owner approval. This note describes engineering
changes; private study inputs and model responses are retained outside Git.

## Diagnosis

The exploratory campaign performs intake and literature work but has no registered
empirical study. Generating additional topics cannot supply the missing experiment.
Malformed hypothesis output could reach downstream workers, while one malformed
historical row prevented consolidation of later valid work. Persisted ladder labels
could disagree with the evidence contract currently applied by the system.

## Implemented repair

- Validate the complete hypothesis/selection contract and finish reason. Permit one
  bounded formatting retry, then stop before downstream work if invalid.
- Admit V2 empirical results only through a source-bound registered verifier that
  replays raw evidence. Count independent study units, not calls or rounds. A diagnostic
  never earns L2; historical non-V2 behavior is retained.
- Record a durable, source-bound research focus with a named next artifact and owner.
  Focus-first mode holds new topic intake while preserving independently eligible
  promotion work. Selecting a focus does not activate a study or inherit a rung.
- Quarantine known invalid consolidation inputs with exact source receipts and process
  later valid rows. Serialize execution, make retries idempotent, and propagate unexpected
  failures. Historical projection uses recorded claims instead of generating new ones.
- Recompute current UI evidence levels from exact sources, showing the historical label
  separately. Withhold ambiguous or missing-source credit. Add focus and next-gate views
  to Now and Research; keep browsing distinct from changing the research priority.
- Treat the permanent Flash deployment as the serving health authority; intentionally
  stopped legacy models are not an outage.
