## 2. What broke (and how)

_Factual list of failures + resolutions. Reflective lessons left for §3._

- **Qwen RAM diagnosis was wrong in shape** (early Phase 0). Initial claim: "vllm-gemma is holding 110 GiB of unified memory." Reality: `docker stats` showed vllm container at 986 MiB userspace; the 117 GiB was vllm's GPU-side KV reservation in unified memory (default `--gpu-memory-utilization 0.9`). Mechanism was wrong; the number was coincidentally right. Caught + corrected before commitment.
- **vllm-qwen launched without `--enable-auto-tool-choice` / `--tool-call-parser qwen3_coder`** in Phase 0a'. First Topic-2 re-run with critic flipped hit 400 errors. SubAgent caught them, critic returned `status=error`, Nara re-dispatched 6× before hitting max_depth=12. Fix: relaunch with tool-call flags + drop `--max-model-len 32768→16384` + bump util `0.2→0.25` to clear KV-cache memory shortage.
- **SubAgent's JSON extraction only looked at `content`** (post-Phase-0). Qwen3.x's reasoning-mode separation routes the answer to `model_extra["reasoning_content"]` on some prompts. Topic 2 Qwen-critic run hit `schema_mismatch` → fallback "survives" (substrate fallback, not actual Qwen verdict). Fix: `4068511` added reasoning_content fallback. Topic 2 third attempt produced a real Qwen verdict (`survives`, identical to Gemma).
- **`tests/test_subagent.py` was silently broken** since D-035's backend refactor. Fixture patched `sa_mod._sync_client` but that attribute was removed when SubAgent switched to backend-registry. 10 tests erroring; not caught because nothing in subsequent commits exercised `test_subagent.py` directly (critic_loop_v0 tests stub `run_subagent` at a higher level). Fixed in `4068511` by patching through `agent_wrapper.wrapper` instead.
- **Delegated UI builder agents twice initially wrote into the main repo checkout** instead of their worktree paths. Both agents caught + reverted themselves before commit; main was always clean. But also: both delegated branches were forked from older HEADs, requiring merge-from-main (round 1) and cherry-pick (round 2) recoveries on the primary session side.
- **Three PDFs unreadable via pypdfium2**: Camerer BGT (encoded font glyphs); Young 1993 and van Damme 1994 (scanned, no text layer — 32 and 91 chars/page respectively). Resolved mid-session via tesseract OCR after the human installed `tesseract-ocr` package.
- **`learning_mutations_and_long.pdf.crdownload`**: filename suggested a partial download; file was actually complete (29 pages, full text extractable). User's Chrome partial-download filename convention preserved.
- **Hypothesize stochastic failure on iter-2026-05-27-005**: returned the raw model output as `text` (instead of parsing out the `chosen` field). Unrelated to critic flip; coincidental. Substrate did NOT have an automatic retry; iter-005 was a wasted slot. Hypothesize's parser is robust to most failures but this stochastic shape leaked through.

## 6. What the next session needs to do — Phase 2 candidates

_Factual proposals; recommendation deferred to §3/§4 reflection._

Per `docs/sources/research_program_v2.md` § "Program Arc" Phase 2 (months 4–9): "Full autoresearch loop operational across synthetic + semi-synthetic tiers; first real findings logged; 1–2 workshop papers submitted." Three candidate next-session anchors:

### Candidate A — Robustness battery on the three Phase-2 topics

**What.** Replay Topics 1 / 2 / 3 five times each with seed / prompt / model-version variations. Tabulate verdict stability per cell (novelty class distribution × critic verdict distribution × top-neighbor doc_id distribution).

**Why.** Today's verdicts are single-sample. We don't know whether Topic 2's `rediscovery` flip is robust or whether one in five runs would have stuck on `novel`. Research_program_v2 § "Robustness as first-class concern" calls this "either an obstacle or its own contribution — systematic study of finding-robustness across prompt/seed/model variation is publishable." Directly maps to § Days 31–60 and § Days 61–90 "systematic robustness study … publishable."

**Acceptance signal.** A table per topic showing modal verdict + variation; either (a) verdicts are stable → confidence in today's reads, or (b) verdicts are unstable → that itself is a Phase-1-preprint candidate per research_program_v2 § Days 61–90.

**Effort.** 5 × 3 = 15 iterations × ~30 s = ~10 min execution + instrumentation to vary seed/prompt and aggregate.

### Candidate B — Semi-synthetic tier (multi-agent LLM societies)

**What.** Build 2–3 designed scenarios where multiple LLM agents play game-theoretic interactions: e.g., a 4-player public goods game with heterogeneous types, a coordination scenario on a small social network, a stag-hunt with reputation signaling. Compare aggregate behavior (cooperation rate, equilibrium selection, coalition formation) to classical game-theoretic predictions where they exist.

**Why.** Research_program_v2 § "Sandbox spectrum" explicitly lists this as Tier 2. The synthetic-tier work (`experiments/exp001_repeated_pd`) is done. Semi-synthetic is the next leap; Park et al. 2023 ("Generative Agents") and Aher / Arriaga / Kalai 2023 are the relevant prior art (already cited in research_program_v2 § Track A). Findings here are publishable workshop-level work per the Phase 2 milestone.

**Acceptance signal.** At least one designed scenario runs end-to-end with reproducible behavior across runs; a finding (positive or negative) emerges that LOOP_V0's literature loop can then evaluate.

**Effort.** Several days to a week. New `experiments/exp002_*` directory likely.

### Candidate C — Auto-seeding from recent arXiv + Step-8 human gate

**What.** Two-part:
(1) Replace human-typed topics in `orchestrator/loop_v0_cli.py` with auto-seeds from recent arXiv ingest (`papers_recent` collection). Nara picks the most-recent-N papers, generates a topic from each.
(2) Add a Step-8 human-gate event to `loop_memory.jsonl`: human reads journal entry, marks `kept_for_followup` / `discarded_as_rediscovery` / `discarded_as_nonsense`. The gate decision is conditioning context for the next iteration's hypothesize step.

**Why.** LOOP_V0 § "Auto-seeding from latest arXiv ingest is a v0.5 add-on, not v0" — explicitly deferred from LOOP_V0 to Phase 2. The Step-8 human gate matches `ARCHITECTURE.md` §6 step 8 and research_program_v2 § "Public research journal as primary data" (the journal is the loop's primary data, but today nothing feeds it back into future iterations). Together they move LOOP_V0 from "single-shot human-triggered" toward "continuous-ish operation" which is the Phase 2 Loop v1 milestone.

**Acceptance signal.** One full day's auto-seeded iterations produce a journal the human reads + dispositions; dispositions show up in the next day's hypothesize context.

**Effort.** Auto-seeding ~half a day. Human-gate plumbing ~half a day. Together a 1-day slice.
