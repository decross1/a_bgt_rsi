## Why the funnel stops

The scientific ladder deliberately asks for novelty and a `survives` critic verdict at L1, then a valid synthetic experiment at L2, replication at L3, adversarial qualification at L4, and an explicit human valid verdict at L5. `thesis_to_experiment.is_eligible` repeats the novelty/critic requirements and routes only six keyword-matched classical games; public goods and stag hunt remain design-only in that constructor. A known mechanism labeled `rediscovery` or an unresolved `undecidable` claim cannot be dispatched through this route. This protects scientific claims, but it also conflates “not new theory” with “not yet tested as a useful application” if that is the only route offered for trading-relevant work. [Evidence ladder](/home/decross1/projects/a_bgt_rsi/workers/evidence_ladder.py), [constructor](/home/decross1/projects/a_bgt_rsi/orchestrator/thesis_to_experiment.py).

The August 19 Docket diagnosis counted 20 open clusters then, 13 blocked on `undecidable`, and zero advanceable through the available action menu. It also found 55 of 58 recent iterations on one topic and four coordinator actions never used in 322 cycles. Those are **historical** measurements, not current counts; the current ledger now has 100 open clusters. The Docket was still a draft and its answer was a router that presents exact actionable work and refuses to invent a path. Recent campaign noops and the newly deferred experiment/market actions show that the “topic to experiment” edge still needs explicit registration; simply generating more speculative topics or relaxing the critic would not produce an empirical result. A critic instrument battery with known-good and known-bad cases is more defensible than turning `undecidable` into `survives`. Earlier measured red-team scaffold changes shifted condemnation heavily at fixed weights, and early Qwen critic parse failures later resolved; protocol/scaffold failures are not model or science verdicts. ## Smallest application-evidence seam

Add **one immutable `applied_trial/v1` manifest and result sidecar**, connected by exact IDs/hashes to an existing mechanism or claim and to a registered application question. Do not edit an activated scientific campaign or alter its L0–L5 verdicts. A direct, read-only paper driver can execute the first trial before Nara gains a new coordinator action; later, a reviewed registration may admit a bounded empirical trial action. The manifest should freeze:

1. `source_mechanism_id`, literature/prior classification, source campaign/iteration or negative-ledger IDs, and the precise reason this is an *application* test rather than new-theory promotion;
2. one falsifiable empirical prediction, market/instrument universe, decision timestamp and outcome horizon, observation and action rules, primary metric, failure/stop rule, and immutable train/validation/out-of-sample split;
3. raw data snapshot IDs and quote timestamps, bid/ask/depth or event-contract executable side, fee/slippage/fill assumptions, latency and availability, plus a matched no-trade/market/naïve baseline;
4. every scheduled paper intent and its actual simulated fill/no-fill status, all errors and not-run cases in the denominator, realized later outcome, costed paper P&L and uncertainty/repeat checks;
5. a separately named application disposition: `not_tested`, `invalid`, `negative`, `paper_supported`, or `needs_replication`, with exact reopening condition and optional human decision. Neither `paper_supported` nor human review is a scientific L5 or a live-trading approval.

The sidecar is the data-model change: it gives known-prior game theory a legitimate empirical test target without bypassing novelty honesty. The driver is a narrow extension of exp007's normalization/scoring seam to **prospective open-market captures** and quote-aware paper decisions; it should not reuse exp007's resolved-fixture BSS/P&L as the baseline. A bounded projection can join manifest → snapshot → paper intent → later outcome by exact IDs, and report missing/ambiguous links as unknown. This follows v2's already specified attempt denominators, immutable preregistration, campaign-lineage rules and separated evaluation graph rather than inventing a second scientific rung. [V2 work unit and measurement contract](/home/decross1/projects/a_bgt_rsi/LOOP_V2.md), [exp007 adapter/scorer](/home/decross1/projects/a_bgt_rsi/experiments/exp007_polymarket/market_data.py), [campaign link contract](/home/decross1/projects/a_bgt_rsi/orchestrator/research_campaign.py).

The first 30 days should produce a *reviewable empirical outcome*, not a promised profitable strategy: in week 1, freeze one known-mechanism hypothesis and start timestamped, read-only market/quote capture; in week 2, preregister a matched baseline and costed paper rule before looking at later outcomes; in week 3, collect prospective decisions and all failures; in week 4, adjudicate an out-of-sample and repeated panel, preserve a negative result if that is what appears, and make a separate owner decision on any later real-trading infrastructure. This schedule is a workflow proposal, not evidence the strategy works. It targets the specific missing edge from topic to testable application while retaining the scientific critic, novelty, and L5 human gates.
