# One next-month quote/abstain control linked to H1

**Status: the protocol below was drafted before execution. Three bounded development arms were subsequently completed; see [the observed results](CONTROLLED_GAME_RESULTS.md). No market labels, orders, or trading-edge evidence.** Keep the existing 12-cell, eight-round public-goods `DESIGN.md` pilot as a planned serving/response *engineering* check; that off-tree implementation has not been validated or run. This is one later applied control under the existing [H1 known-prior transfer](</home/decross1/projects/a_bgt_rsi/bench/applied_trading/h1_topic_transfer.json>), not a second canonical science campaign or a relaxation of L0–L5 novelty and critic gates. The strategic prior is quote adjustment under adverse selection and inventory exposure ([Avellaneda–Stoikov original model](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/HighFrequencyTrading.pdf), [Guéant and coauthors' original inventory model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.3115)).

Freeze a **single-period, risk-neutral supplier decision**. The supplier starts with signed inventory `q ∈ {-2,0,+2}` and chooses `QUOTE` one ask unit or `ABSTAIN`. A disclosed scripted taker schedule supplies one of two information states `F ∈ {low,high}` and known fill probabilities `p(low)=0.4`, `p(high)=0.8`; if realized fills are simulated, their draws and seeds are frozen but withheld until after choice. A fill earns spread premium `s=2` payoff units, suffers adverse mid-price move `A(low)=0.5`, `A(high)=2.5`, and reduces inventory from `q` to `q-1`. Inventory cost is `C(q)=0.75 q²/2`; posting the quote costs `c=0.2` whether or not it fills. Abstaining yields zero incremental payoff and leaves inventory unchanged. The full formula, taker probabilities, state values, one-step horizon and action meanings are disclosed to the focal model; no realized fill or oracle action is shown before its choice:

`E[U(QUOTE)|q,F] = p(F) × [s - A(F) + C(q) - C(q-1)] - c; E[U(ABSTAIN)] = 0.`

The scripted analytical oracle chooses the higher expected payoff, crediting all exact ties if a later registered grid introduces any. With these literal numbers there are no ties:

| Inventory `q` | Low information: quote payoff / best action | High information: quote payoff / best action |
|---:|---|---|
| -2 | -0.35 / ABSTAIN | -2.10 / ABSTAIN |
| 0 | +0.25 / QUOTE | -0.90 / ABSTAIN |
| +2 | +0.85 / QUOTE | +0.30 / QUOTE |

These make falsifiable counterfactuals before model output: at `q=0`, moving from low to high adverse-selection information switches quote to abstain despite a higher fill probability; at high information, moving from short to long inventory eventually switches abstain to quote. Reverse the neutral printed state labels `low/high` and action labels `A/B` while preserving **numeric parameters** as a label-permutation negative control: oracle actions must stay attached to payoffs, not words or label order. A second negative control replaces `A(high)=2.5` with `0.5` at fixed `p(high)=0.8`: the precomputed oracle then quotes at `q=0` (+0.70) but still abstains at `q=-2` (-0.50). This isolates the adverse-move value from the word “high” or fill probability alone. Freeze these controls and expected actions before any prompt or policy change.

Use a small no-feedback arithmetic comprehension set with *disjoint* numeric values to finalize parsing/prompts, then freeze an evaluation grid withheld from prompt-tuning calls: the six `(q,F)` states × four declared seed/order/neutral-label replicates, plus the two changed-adverse-move cells (`q=0,-2` at high information) × four replicates. Run all 32 scheduled choice calls under one qualified local endpoint, literal generation policy, finite per-call cap and controller restoration deadline. Counterbalance order and labels deterministically in the manifest. Public metrics are **optimal choices per scheduled cell and per valid cell, valid-cell expected-payoff regret, invalid/timeout rate, and attempted/scheduled coverage**; private SHA-bound raw responses and replayed oracle calculations support independent grading. An invalid or missing action gets no invented quote/abstain payoff or regret; its cell stays in the scheduled denominator, and a partial window cannot be called complete or a positive treatment. Compare counterfactual consistency across paired numeric states and label permutations; do not select a subset after seeing answers. This is an ability-to-respond-to-disclosed-payoffs test, not an equilibrium, multiagent cooperation effect, or market forecast.

The connection to the prospective H1 data is **a bounded transfer question**, not a causal bridge: does a supplier-like rule that prices adverse-selection and inventory costs choose to abstain in the controlled game, and does the independently frozen H1 proxy (prior-hour signed buy flow jointly with visible ask-side L5 size contraction) improve a later reference-quote diagnostic over a matched momentum/exposure/cost baseline? Current five-minute REST L5 snapshots have neither participant identity nor supplier inventory, informedness, order-event sequence, fills or executable price proof. Therefore no market row may be assigned `q`, `p(F)` or `A(F)` from REST data, and a successful controlled-game best response cannot promote the H1 pilot to `paper_supported`, a new theory, or a real trade. A later strict forward application needs source-admitted book-event/quote provenance and fresh matched negative controls. A negative control-game result diagnoses model/payoff-response capability; a negative H1 result falsifies the chosen market proxy on its registered observations. Keep those dispositions separate.
