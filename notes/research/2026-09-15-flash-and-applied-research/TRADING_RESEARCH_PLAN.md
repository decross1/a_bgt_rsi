# Strategic Market Behavior: a 30-Day Paper-Trading Research Program

The attainable one-month result is a **runnable, timestamped paper-trading research pipeline**, a sealed prospective evaluation, and either a small set of active paper-trade intents or an honest decision to abstain. A profitable live trading system is not an evidence-supported promise. Real orders, exchange accounts, wallet signing, paid feeds, and a jurisdiction-specific venue decision are outside this program. Five- and fifteen-minute flow predictions can test a market mechanism, but retail taker fees plus spread may swamp their move. The first *actionable* spot paper-intent candidate should therefore be a separately preregistered **one-hour or four-hour state-conditioned entry/exit**, with short-horizon flow used as an explanatory and timing input only if it adds incremental net value. This remains low-frequency research on the existing Spark lab, not a millisecond queue-position contest.

The project has a formal game-theory research campaign, but it is studying payoff comprehension and utility-consistent best responses to scripted opponents in repeated public-goods games. Its twelve initial cells are an engineering pilot, and `run_experiment` and `forecast_markets` remain deferred.[1] That work can explain mechanisms and evaluate agents; it is not a market signal. A bounded September 15 pipeline audit found 100 open idea clusters (97 L0, three L1) among 220 distinct created clusters, with no current campaign-linked surfaced finding in the inspected ledger. The current campaign-linked iteration was recorded as rediscovery/critic-undecidable, and its idea cluster was later killed for `paper_prior_exists`.[20] These are point-in-time project facts, not proof that the science pipeline cannot advance. The existing Polymarket `exp007` result is also not a trading baseline: its committed `BELOW_MARKET` forecast is an offline `MOCK_LLM` run on already-resolved markets, and its hypothetical stakes use an implied probability rather than a contemporaneous executable quote, depth, fee, or fill.[2] The adapter can read Gamma `bestBid` and `bestAsk` as a midpoint fallback, but its normalized row retains only `implied_prob`; that detail does not repair the retrospective design. Existing negative results and research artifacts should remain intact. A known theory can motivate a separately preregistered empirical trial without being relabeled a novel theory; a novelty gate rejecting rediscovery should not be made to adjudicate whether an as-of market application passes its own falsification tests.

This would reduce historical depth, so the initial backtest would be a modest candle/trade comparator rather than a simulated historical order-book strategy. Binance COIN-M's public `BTCUSD_PERP` funding-rate history, mark/index premium and open-interest statistics are available as potential *stress indicators* for a BTC spot model; the latter statistics are documented with a latest-30-days limit. They are not interchangeable with USD-M instruments and do not imply that derivatives trading is permitted.[13] Published research links crypto carry and arbitrage constraints to crash risk, while original work also warns that exchange-reported open interest can be inconsistent. Those are reasons to test a noisy crowding proxy, not proof of a profitable signal.[14]

## Four falsifiable hypotheses, only two primary paper-trade lanes

**H1: strategic liquidity and taker-flow imbalance, spot.** The mechanism is that aggressive buying or selling can consume nearby liquidity and that displayed liquidity can replenish asymmetrically. Original order-book research found a strong relation between best-quote order-flow imbalance and short-horizon price changes in equities; transfer to crypto, to minutes rather than ticks, and to net profit is entirely unproven.[15] On Binance history, reconstruct *trade-only* signed volume from aggregated trades, plus lagged return, volume and volatility. Its `m` field identifies whether the buyer was maker, which gives an aggressor-side proxy, **not informed-trader identity**.[3] Register one simple flow model and compare it with no-trade, lagged-return/momentum, and volatility-only baselines. In a separate prospective enrichment, add measured spread, top-book imbalance, quote depletion and replenishment only after forward-capture passes sequence and freshness checks. The five-/fifteen-minute next-midprice target tests **mechanism and forecasting**, not a primary trading edge. The **separate one-/four-hour paper-intent test** asks whether a measured liquidity state (depletion versus replenishment, signed taker flow, spread) changes the net outcome of a fixed position-timing rule **beyond the same rule using lagged momentum/volatility alone**. Freeze the state rule and holding period; do not relabel generic momentum as game theory. Before issuing any spot intent, require the model's expected favorable move to exceed the known fee floor plus measured spread, decision latency and slippage sensitivity. Reject the actionable arm if its incremental net improvement vanishes in forward days, under doubled costs, or under a sign shuffle within day, even if the short-horizon directional classifier succeeds.

**H2: adverse-selection and inventory filter, spot diagnostic.** Inventory-risk models and maker-flow-toxicity literature describe why a passive quote can be hit just before an adverse price move; they are a useful strategic frame, but the lab lacks queue position, order acknowledgments and actual fills.[16] A prospective study may record markout after trades, quote depletion and spread widening, then test whether an H1 taker signal deteriorates in toxic/high-volatility periods. Compare within matched spread/volatility bins and with a time-shifted placebo. Do **not** compute a maker P&L or rebate from an observed midpoint: a displayed bid or ask does not establish the lab's place in the queue. The result can be a risk gate or a negative finding, not a market-making launch.

**H3: strategic event repricing, Polymarket.** For a bounded set of liquid, still-unresolved binary markets, record the full question, resolution source and rules, closing schedule, YES/NO token mapping, contemporaneous CLOB order book, fee information, source timestamp, receipt timestamp and any publicly available event information seen by the forecaster. A model or manually specified base-rate forecast `p` is useful only when it was frozen **before** the outcome and measured against a valid ask for a hypothetical purchase, with enough depth for the fixed paper stake. Market midpoint/price-history is a forecast comparator, not a paper execution price.[10][11] A YES paper intent requires a conservative expected payoff after ask and applicable taker fee; NO uses its own token ask, not `1 - YES midpoint`. On resolution, compare Brier score to the *contemporaneous* market-implied baseline and realized net paper payoff; report both, because good probability calibration does not itself create a tradable edge. Polymarket documents category-dependent taker fees and per-market fee parameters, so query the fee applicable to each market/time rather than hard-code an account-wide number.[12] Events unresolved by day 30 are censored for settlement claims; their mark-to-market is separately labeled. Resolution can be delayed or disputed, and the market title alone does not settle the contract.[17] Reject the lane if source information leaks post-decision facts, if the apparent edge is concentrated near known outcomes, or if asks/depth/fees remove it.

**H4: crowding and funding stress, spot diagnostic.** Test whether lagged COIN-M funding/premium and open interest identify a regime in which H1's net outcomes worsen. The test is an interaction or risk filter on the same spot paper ledger, not a futures position. Predefine the lag/availability timestamp, account for missing records and compare with a lagged volatility/momentum regime. Discard if the incremental result fails forward days or another accessible spot venue; do not fit a broad factor grid to a 30-day period.[13][14]

The first month should prioritize **H3 event repricing and H1's one-/four-hour state-conditioned timing arm** as paper-intent candidates. H1's five-/fifteen-minute classifier, H2 and H4 are mechanism/risk diagnostics unless net-value evidence says otherwise. A low-frequency program leaves space to validate source timestamps and execution assumptions, whereas a high-frequency quoting strategy would require exchange-specific latency, queue, partial-fill, cancel and inventory measurement that the current apparatus cannot supply. Inventory-control and order-flow theories are **known mechanisms**; the empirical question is whether a preregistered, source-bound implementation adds forward value at this venue and horizon. It need not claim theoretical novelty.[15][16]

## What an honest dataset and paper fill require

Each immutable observation should bind `venue`, `symbol` or `condition_id`/token, UTC `exchange_timestamp`, local `received_timestamp`, raw response or WS-frame SHA-256, source URL/channel, sequence or update IDs, capture-process version, gap status and, for a quote, book-validity proof. Raw Binance ZIP plus its official checksum and retrieval time should be retained; the same historical period must not be quietly replaced if the archive revises a file.[3] Coinbase heartbeats/sequence and Kraken CRC are corresponding gap detectors; Binance's diff-depth snapshot rules are a reconstruction prerequisite.[4][6][9] A signal created at time `t` may read only facts received and effective by `t`. A paper taker entry occurs at the **next** valid displayed ask or bid after a fixed observation/decision latency, with a size cap below displayed depth; its exit uses a subsequent valid opposite quote. A gap, stale quote, insufficient size, disconnected feed, fee uncertainty or unavailable market is `not_run`/unfillable, never a zero-cost fill.

For a spot long, compute the hypothetical cash-flow return from `bid_exit / ask_entry - 1`, then deduct entry and exit fee rates and conservative extra slippage. This is more realistic than close-to-close returns or `mid_exit / mid_entry - 1` because the spread is paid. Binance's published ordinary reference fee is 0.1% per trade; **two taker legs alone imply roughly 0.2%, or 20 basis points, before spread and extra slippage**. This arithmetic is a useful pre-registered *kill check* for a short-horizon strategy, not a measured BTC/ETH spread or realized fee. The actual user's tier, symbol promotions and execution route are not known, so use the published reference only as a scenario and report at least base and doubled-fee/slippage cases.[18] Reject any paper intent whose conservative predicted move is at or below that *measured total* threshold; classify a five-minute directional improvement that cannot clear it as explanatory only. For Coinbase/Kraken, inspect the chosen venue's published fee schedule at implementation time and use a conservative scenario; no account-specific rate can be inferred from a public feed. For a prediction-market paper purchase, record the token ask, shares available at that price, the market's fee parameters and total cash outlay. A positive expected value at a midpoint that disappears at the ask is a rejection, not an opportunity. No maker rebates or hypothetical queue fills enter the primary score.[11][12]

The model/data join should be simple enough to audit: a source receipt, an as-of feature row, a prediction/abstention, a delayed executable-quote simulation, an exit/settlement row and a score row. Keep all attempted predictions and timeouts in denominators. A trading model that returns no valid response or abstains should affect coverage; silently selecting only completed profitable-looking predictions would change the estimand. Save private model completions for independent regrading and expose only content-free aggregate metrics. The existing lab's role/model benchmark program can provide an independent inference-quality receipt, but a strong general model score must not be projected into trading return.

## Chronological design and decision rule

**Days 1–3, source and preregistration.** Confirm which unauthenticated endpoint is actually reachable under applicable terms; choose at most BTC and ETH spot on one primary venue, one backup venue for replication if feasible, and a bounded liquid binary-event list with known rules. Freeze symbol/token-selection criteria before looking at future outcomes. Start a read-only capture of trades, quotes/books, market metadata, event rules, fees, timestamps and feed gaps. Register H1–H4: five/fifteen minutes for flow-mechanism forecasts, **one/four hours for a separate cost-gated state-conditioned spot paper-intent test**, execution latency, fixed paper size and cost assumptions. The Polymarket adapter needs a prospective data contract that retains bid/ask/depth rather than only `implied_prob`. Do not call the old resolved-market experiment a baseline.[2][10]

**Days 4–10, small historical comparator.** A bounded default is **60 complete pre-start UTC days** of the official BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT Spot archive, if obtainable under the operator's actual access conditions: 40 earliest days for development, 20 later days for one sealed validation, then the new day-11-to-30 forward capture as a distinct test. Do not choose the period after viewing labels; receipt exact ZIP/checksum hashes and mark absent days. One-hour observations yield at most about 1,440 hourly rows per symbol before gaps; four-hour outcomes overlap, so the independent unit is a day/session, not each row. Build only features genuinely derivable from those files: signed trade imbalance, lagged returns, volatility and volume. The split must be chronological; embargo at least the longest four-hour label horizon when windows overlap. Fit no more than a registered simple linear/logistic flow model, a persistence/momentum baseline and a no-signal baseline, with a tiny recorded parameter search. The one-/four-hour timing variant must be compared with **the same holding period and exposure cap** but no liquidity-state feature, so any added result is attributable to the strategic state rather than generic momentum. Any early captured L2 is a separate development sample, not a way to synthesize L2 before capture began. Seal code, input hashes, rules and thresholds before the forward test. Historical trades/candles alone can evaluate predictive direction and a rough **reference-cost** sensitivity, but not an executable-book backtest.

**Days 11–20, prospective shadow decisions.** Produce frozen five-/fifteen-minute H1 mechanism predictions but issue H1 spot paper-intents only from the separately cost-gated one-/four-hour arm; enter only at a subsequent valid quote, include every timeout/abstention/source outage, and retain rejected intents with reasons. Capture H2/H4 variables as predeclared diagnostics. For H3, issue forecasts only on unresolved markets and bind each to a quote, rule and information cutoff. Do not tune against this forward window. Monitor availability, not just return.

**Days 21–30, continuation and assessment.** Continue the same sealed forward protocol. Report spot net paper bps per completed round trip, daily or session-clustered uncertainty, turnover, exposure, worst paper drawdown, spread/fee/slippage contribution, prediction coverage, quote validity and number of intended versus fillable decisions. For events, report Brier skill versus contemporaneous quote-derived base rate on **resolved** events, market/event-clustered uncertainty if enough independent resolutions exist, paper net payoff and censored unresolved count. Multiple signals within a day or one event are dependent; do not treat them as independent replications or quote an impressive Sharpe from a tiny selected set. Original work on testing hundreds of return factors and backtest overfitting supplies the reason to disclose all tried variants and preserve untouched forward data.[19] The forward duration can create an operational proof in a month; it is rarely enough to establish durable excess return. Report low sample/coverage as `unknown`.

The day-30 paper gate should require: (a) complete source and fee/quote provenance for scored fills; (b) a sealed chronological forward window with all attempts counted; (c) improvement against the frozen no-trade or simple baseline, **after base and doubled costs**; (d) direction surviving sign/time placebo and a small universe or venue sensitivity; and (e) sufficient independent days/events to make the effect interpretable. A practical *operational coverage* floor to preregister is at least **15 distinct valid forward days** and **20 fillable, decision-before-quote spot opportunities spread over at least ten days**; the numerical floor is a proposed minimum for a functioning paper pipeline, not a significance guarantee. For the event lane, preregister a minimum count of **independently resolved events** before declaring probability skill; if fewer resolve within 30 days, report `unknown` rather than converting censored events into wins. Freeze effect thresholds, maximum tolerated source gaps, paper-size/depth and minimum counts before evaluation. If the source/cost/falsification gates fail, publish the negative or unknown trial and run zero active paper trades. If they pass, continue a bounded read-only paper-intent ledger with a provisional `paper_supported` result; neither outcome authorizes real transactions.

## Smallest project integration

The formal V2 campaign and science ladder can continue to study agent strategic behavior. A **separate immutable `applied_trial/v1` sidecar** should link a known mechanism/prior citation and any relevant campaign iteration to a market hypothesis, source manifest, prospective quote record, cost rule, split manifest, all-outcome ledger and falsification result. Use the local audit's proposed dispositions `not_tested`, `invalid`, `negative`, `paper_supported` or `needs_replication`, distinct from L1–L5 scientific novelty/promotion.[20] This avoids donating a retrospective market result or a killed novel-theory claim to a trading edge, while allowing a useful empirical application of a known mechanism. No source writer should add an order endpoint or wallet/auth surface in this first month. The minimal executable stages are read-only `capture`, `asof_features`, `predict`, `paper_intent`, `paper_fill`, `score` and `negative_controls`; each reads bounded immutable manifests and writes its own receipt. The project may reuse its existing read-only Gamma adapter and paper-scoring code only after preserving raw executable quotes, rule/fee provenance and decision-before-outcome chronology.[2] Operational registration of any new external data capture and lab resource use belongs to the owner/root; this research report does not grant a new external budget.
