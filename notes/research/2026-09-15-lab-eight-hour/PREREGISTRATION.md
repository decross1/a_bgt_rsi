## Decision rules established before new outcomes

The default remains **RETAIN-INCUMBENT**. A whole-lab replacement recommendation
requires a complete admitted paired panel, fresh confirmation, verified exact
restoration and no unresolved memory, tool/parser or provenance failure.

Report task families separately. The 48 topic tasks must not dominate a pooled
claim about scientific ability. Report equal-family mean accuracy as a secondary
summary, individual family success counts, and correct tasks per total evaluation
hour including failed attempts. Report cold start and restoration separately.

For an upgrade recommendation, fresh coding and science success must each be at
least the resident arm's, no primary task family may lose more than ten absolute
percentage points, and the equal-family primary accuracy must not regress.
Additionally require either at least 15% better correct-task throughput on the
combined science/coding tasks or higher science/coding accuracy with no more than
25% additional evaluation wall time. These are engineering selection thresholds,
not a claim of statistical significance. With few fresh items, a near tie stays
inconclusive and retains the incumbent. Inspect every paired disagreement and
cluster uncertainty by source task where repeated treatments share a problem.

A specialized role recommendation may be narrower than a whole-lab replacement.
It must identify the winning role and its evidence, rather than turning one
science gain into an unsupported all-role promotion. No production cutover is
performed by the evaluator.
