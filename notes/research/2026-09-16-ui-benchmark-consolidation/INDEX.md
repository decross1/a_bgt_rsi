# Research workspace and benchmark consolidation

Owner-authorized window: **2026-09-16 03:41:19–11:41:19 UTC**.

This work repairs the current research experience and creates a repeatable
benchmark program. Research execution, accepted scientific findings, model
quality, runtime reliability and trading performance remain distinct claims.

## Work lanes

1. **Research workspace:** selected-record evidence, criticism, learning and
   next test; bounded archives; simpler navigation; removal of redundant UI.
2. **Stable benchmark:** a small, prospectively frozen core with separate
   capability and actor–tool–critic harness outcomes, comparable versions and explicit
   expiry. Historical exploratory panels remain diagnostics.
3. **Flash closure:** one final registered output-cap diagnostic, retaining the
   existing production pair unless independent promotion evidence supports a
   different decision.
4. **Applied agenda:** investigate options first where the game-theoretic
   mechanism and observable data fit; prediction markets and justified crypto
   remain alternatives. Existing BTC records remain historical evidence.

desktop, narrow, dark, keyboard and detail states. The original images and DOM
receipts are local artifacts under
`a_bgt_rsi_v2_artifacts/2026-09-16/ui-benchmark-eight-hour/ui-audit/`.
