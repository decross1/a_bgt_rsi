The **known strategic prior** is that suppliers of liquidity adjust quotes when inventory and adverse-selection exposure change. If buy-side takers may be informed or keep hitting asks, an ask-side supplier may raise, withdraw or size down displayed offers to control that risk; competing suppliers may replenish instead. This is a proposed transfer from dealer quote/inventory models and order-flow work, not an identified fact about the BTCUSDT participants. [Avellaneda–Stoikov original model](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/HighFrequencyTrading.pdf), [Guéant and coauthors' original inventory model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.3115), [Cont, Kukanov and Stoikov's original book-event price-impact paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402). Flow-toxicity proxies have material empirical critiques; a signed-flow statistic should never be labeled an informed-trader classifier. [Original critique](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1881731).

The **observable H1 prediction** is narrower: at a frozen closed-hour decision, does a jointly gated condition of previously received buy-taker imbalance and a decrease in displayed ask-side L5 size improve a later one-hour forecast and fixed long/flat *reference-quote diagnostic* beyond a genuinely matched intercept, momentum, spread-cost, hold time and exposure policy? The current forward feature rows have no lagged volatility or total-volume control, so this pilot cannot isolate every mechanical price-impact explanation. The current `applied_trial/v1` rule has additive flow and depletion terms plus conjunctive minimum thresholds; it has **no literal flow-by-depletion coefficient**. Its candidate and baseline intercept/momentum weights are separately declared, so a matched diagnostic must check their equality before attributing an incremental result. A literal interaction would need a separate preregistration. The historical 60-day comparator can test *lagged signed flow alone* against momentum/volatility/volume; it has no historical book state. The forward feature field currently called `ask_depletion` is only **visible ask-side size contraction across different REST L5 snapshots**. If best prices or levels move, the compared five levels need not be the same orders. That proxy cannot classify fills versus cancellations or infer whose inventory changed. Binance aggregate trade `m` gives an aggressor-side proxy, not buyer identity or informedness. [Implemented trade-only comparator](</home/decross1/projects/a_bgt_rsi_worktrees/flash-followon-20260915/bench/applied_trading/trade_only_baseline.py>), [forward feature builder](</home/decross1/projects/a_bgt_rsi_worktrees/flash-followon-20260915/bench/applied_trading/prospective_hourly_features.py>), [official archive format](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data).

The test separates three statements:

1. **Predictive statement:** the jointly gated signed-flow/visible-ask-size condition improves untouched forward one-hour score against an actually matched intercept/momentum baseline, with every scheduled opportunity and unavailable source counted. Other mechanical price-impact, volatility and volume explanations remain unexcluded until those as-of controls are separately frozen.
2. **Reference-quote statement:** the same frozen rule improves net *displayed-quote diagnostic* bps per scheduled opportunity after **post-decision** REST ask and bid observations, ordinary-reference fees, observed spread/depth/latency and doubled cost sensitivity. This is not an executable fill or strict paper result. A better direction classifier that does not clear this cost/coverage test remains explanatory.
3. **Mechanism statement:** a surviving joint condition is *consistent with* a strategic supply response under the prior models. Five-minute REST snapshots, no participant IDs, no dealer inventory, no queue, no cancellation/fill sequence and no exogenous shock do **not** identify agent incentives or establish causal dealer behavior. A strategically motivated model may be useful even if this stronger interpretation is unavailable.

Freeze two small falsifiers for a later full forward sidecar: (a) permute the *prior-hour* signed-flow signs within UTC-day/lagged-volatility strata **only after** a timestamped lagged-volatility field is registered; (b) remove the flow/depletion terms and conjunctive gate while retaining the exact candidate's intercept, momentum, exposure and cost policy as the matched baseline. These are planned controls, **not executed by the 2–5h REST pilot**. A positive claim should not depend on one anomalous day, post-decision receipts, or 5-/15-minute directional movement too small for two fee legs. Cluster uncertainty by day because four-hour and hourly outcomes overlap. A bad source gap, no usable subsequent displayed quote, a nonpositive incremental net under base/doubled costs, or a joint condition that fails the matched forward test yields `invalid`, `negative`, or `needs_replication`, never a silently selected win. [Original multi-testing warning](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20592).
