# Prospective Mia arm of the known-opponent utility pilot

This arm repeats the admitted Gemma pilot's exact 12 ordered cells: three
committed opponent scripts, own and joint material-payoff objectives, focal
seats 0 and 3, neutral A/B rule labels, one arithmetic check and eight serial
actions per cell. The schedule contains 108 possible requests, 96 of them
action requests. Each later action prompt reveals only actual prior actions
and payoffs; the independent exact dynamic-programming oracle stays private.

The model intervention is the registered optimized reduced47K MTP3 Mia
profile, with a distinct admitted qualification parent and artifact. Sampling
is deliberately held to the Gemma pilot's exact request policy:
`enable_thinking=false`, temperature 0, top-p 1, top-k 64, seed base 301,
maximum 64 output tokens and 30 seconds per request. This policy is an
instrument match; it is not a claim that it is Mia's preferred operating
policy. A result may differ because model, runtime, epoch or stochastic
execution differ. The original Gemma grades and receipts remain unchanged.

The primary measurements are strict arithmetic-comprehension passes out of
12, valid action calls out of 96 scheduled slots, fully observed episodes
out of 12, and zero-regret episodes out of those fully observed. Report
descriptive counts by opponent script, objective and seat. A first invalid
action ends that episode; its remaining actions and full-horizon regret are
unknown. No missing request or partial episode is scored as a failure of
strategy. A full 12-cell schedule may be an admitted empirical observation
even when some episodes have unknown regret. An incomplete schedule is
observed but not admitted as the complete panel.

The supervised worker ceiling is 3,000 seconds: up to 1,500 for source/model
verification, startup and live canaries, up to 900 for the pilot, and a
reserved 600 for exact restoration. The parent may need an additional 600
seconds for emergency restoration after a failed worker; that path is
incomplete, never an admitted panel. The controller requires the shared
resource lease, 20 GiB MemAvailable minimum with continuous sampling,
exact source and process identity, boot-specific canary replay, raw private
SSE/metadata, independent payoff/action/DP replay and clean terminal
supervision before publishing numeric results.

This is a local, disclosed synthetic behavior panel. It is neither an
original-126 benchmark rerun nor a fresh population sample, equilibrium or
novelty proof, or evidence for a trading outcome. Cross-model differences
remain descriptive until a separately registered replicated study addresses
sampling and episode variability. The user retains the scientific claim gate.
