**On the gap between "works" and "works reliably."** [WRITE: the heart
of the post (200–250 words). A 1.00 invocation rate at temperature 0.3
on a single canonical prompt does not prove tool calling "works" in the
sense that matters for the research apparatus — it proves the easy
case works. The real risk is not that the model never calls the tool;
it is that under a slightly different system prompt, or a slightly
broader tool surface, the rate drops to 0.6 and a downstream
observation gets contaminated with a hallucinated payoff. The day's
discipline was building the measurement that would surface that
silently — every record schema-valid, the rate computed before any
prompt-tuning lever was pulled, no `guided_json` patched in to make the
number look better. Tie this to the program's robustness-as-first-class
principle: the test that tells you the apparatus is degrading is more
load-bearing than the trick that makes today's number cleaner.]
